Craig Buchanan's Wish List for Gen Con Indy 2026
Your Wish List contains events that you would like to get tickets for. Your Wish List is different from your cart because tickets selected in your Wish List are only requests, you are not guaranteed to get them until they are added to your cart. Once your Wish List has been processed, the tickets will be placed in your cart and will need to be purchased within two hours. Items in your cart will expire after two hours of inactivity.

Tickets cannot be added to your cart until Event Registration opens, but tickets can be added to your Wish List now.

All of the requested tickets in your Wish List can be processed and added to your cart, if available, in one operation.



When Event Registration opens the button to submit your Wish List will be enabled.

Tickets for events in your Wish List will be processed in order of priority starting with 1, and then 2, and so on. Assign smaller priority numbers to the events that are most important to you.

You can request tickets for a maximum of 50 events in your Wish List.

Click on the event's title to see who you're requesting to get a ticket for.
